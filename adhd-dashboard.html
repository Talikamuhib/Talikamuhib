ADHD Patient Pathway Analytics

An interactive analytics dashboard exploring referral demand, waiting times, appointment outcomes, and clinical service performance across an ADHD care pathway. Built to help service leads understand demand, bottlenecks, and where capacity is needed — designed to support service planning, not clinical diagnosis.

Key Insights

  • Rising demand: Referral volume grows month-on-month, driven mainly by routine referrals.
  • Prioritisation works: Urgent cases are seen far sooner (~30 days) than routine referrals (~85 days).
  • Lost capacity: A 17% did-not-attend rate represents wasted clinical slots — a clear target for reminder and engagement interventions.
  • Access pressure: Only 77% of referrals meet the 18-week access standard, revealing waiting-list pressure concentrated in specific clinics.

Built with synthetic demonstration data (1,200 referrals, 2024–2025). For portfolio and methodology demonstration only — not derived from real patients and not for clinical use.
