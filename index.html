Dashboard 06

Gilgit-Baltistan Climate Trends & Glacial-Melt Risk

An interactive machine-learning dashboard analysing 36 years (1984–2019) of temperature and precipitation from seven Gilgit-Baltistan weather stations to quantify warming and highlight glacial-melt risk.

Fits per-station regressions to measure warming (avg +0.59 °C/decade, fastest at Skardu +2.20 °C/decade), maps trends geographically, groups stations with K-Means, flags anomalous years, and projects future values. Built with Python and Streamlit on Pakistan Meteorological Department records — a demonstration of climate-risk analytics, not an operational forecast.

  • Python
  • Scikit-learn
  • Streamlit
  • Linear Regression
  • K-Means
  • Geospatial
  • Climate Analytics
